# The World in One Day — Tuesday, 17 November 2026

A one-page newspaper front page summing up world news of **Tuesday,
17 November 2026**, for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261117)
"Sum up 17 November's world news in one image".

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the image the
repository holds, `entry.png`, to `/output/entry.png`. It uses no network
and writes the same bytes on every run.

## The six stories in the image

1. **Europe — Ukraine strikes the Crimean Bridge again; span collapses.**
   Second straight day of strikes on the Kerch Bridge; Kyiv says a road
   span collapsed, two people were killed, road traffic was suspended and
   rail resumed. Russia hit Kyiv's bridges over the Dnipro the night before.
2. **Middle East — Iran fires drones at a U.S. base in Bahrain.**
   The attack on the radar at Sheikh Isa base was intercepted with no
   casualties or damage; tankers were hit by mines near the Strait of Hormuz.
3. **Asia — Xi, Trump and Putin set for Thursday talks.** The three leaders
   are to meet in Shenzhen on the sidelines of the APEC leaders' meeting.
4. **Markets — Nasdaq falls again; Nvidia beats estimates.** The Nasdaq fell
   0.7%, its third straight decline, as AI-chip jitters spread; Nvidia
   reported third-quarter earnings after the bell and beat estimates.
   Gold sat near $3,850, about a one-year low.
5. **Asia — Japan scrambles fighters after an airspace violation.** Tokyo
   protested after suspected Russian aircraft entered its airspace.
6. **United States — Pentagon fails its ninth consecutive financial audit.**
   Auditors could not verify $4.8 trillion in assets.

Facts are as reported on 17 November 2026 (Reuters and other outlets).
The pictures are illustrations generated for this entry, not photographs
of the events; the headlines and captions carry the facts.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
# writes out/entry.png (1024x1024 PNG, under 1 MiB)
```
